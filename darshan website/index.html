Must-Visit Holy Destinations in Ayodhya Dham

Discover the most sacred temples, ancient palaces, and serene ghats of Lord Shri Ram’s birthplace with guided assistance.

Major Temple

Shree Ram Janmabhoomi Mandir

6:00 AM - 10:00 PM Free Entry

The magnificent 3-tier Nagara temple of Lord Ram Lalla. Experience divine morning/evening Aarti and marvel at the sandstone carving craftsmanship.

Major Temple

Hanuman Garhi Temple

5:00 AM - 11:00 PM 76 Steps

Ancient fortress temple of Lord Hanuman who protects Ayodhya. Pilgrims traditionally seek his blessing before visiting Ram Janmabhoomi.

Holy Ghat

Sarayu River & Evening Aarti

Aarti at 6:30 PM Boat Ride

Witness the grand evening Maha Aarti with hundreds of lit brass lamps and chanting along Ram Ki Paidi ghats. Enjoy divine sunset boat rides.

Historical Palace

Kanak Bhawan (Sita Ram Palace)

8:00 AM - 9:00 PM Golden Crowns

The golden palace gifted to Devi Sita by Queen Kaikeyi. Famous for beautiful idols adorned with pure gold jewelry and quiet prayer atmosphere.

Holy Ghat

Guptar Ghat (Moksha Dham)

Open 24 Hours Peaceful Riverfront

The sacred ghat where Lord Ram took Jal Samadhi to return to Vaikuntha. Newly renovated riverfront with beautiful gardens and serene breeze.

Major Temple

Nageshwarnath Shiva Temple

5:00 AM - 8:30 PM Lord Shiva

Established by Lord Ram’s son Prince Kush. Dedicated to Lord Shiva, this temple is located right next to Ram Ki Paidi.

Help & Clarity

Frequently Asked Questions

Got questions regarding Ram Mandir entry timings, locker rules, or guide booking? Find quick answers below.

What are the daily timings for Shree Ram Lalla Darshan?

Ram Lalla Darshan is open daily in two slots: 6:00 AM to 12:00 PM in the morning and 4:00 PM to 10:00 PM in the evening. Entry queues generally close 30 minutes before time slots.

Are mobile phones and bags allowed inside Ram Janmabhoomi premises?

No. Mobile phones, electronic gadgets, smartwatches, leather belts, wallets, and large bags are strictly prohibited inside temple premises. Free secure locker facilities are provided at Sugriva Kila where Rahul Yadav helps you store belongings safely.

How does Rahul Yadav assist senior citizens and differently-abled pilgrims?

Rahul Yadav coordinates free wheelchair assistance and golf cart transport from the entry gate up to the main temple pathway, ensuring senior citizens avoid long walking stress.

How do I reach Ayodhya Dham from Airport or Railway Station?

Ayodhya has Maharishi Valmiki International Airport (AYJ) and Ayodhya Dham Junction (AY). We provide direct pick-and-drop service in private AC cabs or local e-rickshaws directly to your hotel or Ram Path.

How can I confirm my tour booking with DM Consultancy?

You can fill out the online booking form on this site or call/WhatsApp Rahul Yadav directly at +91 94511 23456. You will receive an instant booking summary and pickup details.
