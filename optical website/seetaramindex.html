Available Diagnostic Exams

A Legacy of Eye Care

Seetaram Eye Care & Optical

Precision, Quality & Service Since 2001

Seetaram Eye Care & Optical was founded with a singular, powerful mission: to make world-class optical services and premium global eyewear accessible to the people of Ayodhya. What started as a simple, dedicated clinic has evolved into a premier destination for eye health and designer optics, mirroring the legacy and resources of institutions like GKB.

We believe that selecting eyewear should be a sensory, highly personalized experience. Our flagship showroom in Ayodhya blends state-of-the-art clinical testing technology with a luxurious showcase of international brands, ensuring you never have to compromise on vision correction or personal style.

From authorized lens integrations (ZEISS SmartLife, Essilor Varilux) to handpicked frames from Ray-Ban, Oakley, and Prada, Seetaram Eye Care is committed to providing lifetime adjustments, absolute prescription guarantees, and exceptional patient care.
